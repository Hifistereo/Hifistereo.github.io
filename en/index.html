KidMindPath: five free, ad-free educational games for children aged 2–7

KidMindPath (kidmindpath.com) is a free collection of five educational browser games for young children, roughly ages 2 to 7. The games open directly in a browser — there is nothing to download and no account to create. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and every bit of a child's progress stays on that child's own device.

The games are in Latvian. That is the one thing worth knowing before you send a child here: the spoken and written language throughout is Latvian, so the site suits a Latvian-speaking family. The one exception is Mācāmies angliski! ("Let's learn English"), which teaches English vocabulary to Latvian-speaking children. This page exists so that people reading in English can find out what the site is; the site itself is not translated.

The facts, briefly

  • What it is: a hub of five educational web games for young children.
  • Ages: roughly 2–7, with a narrower range per game.
  • Language: Latvian.
  • Price: free. No in-app purchases, no paid tier, no free trial.
  • Ads: none, in any of the five games.
  • Accounts: none. No sign-up, no email address.
  • Data collection: none. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts. There is no server to send anything to.
  • Where progress lives: in the browser's own storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) on the child's device. A parent can erase all of it with one button.
  • Requirements: any phone, tablet or computer with a modern browser. Nothing to install.
  • Who makes it: an independent, non-commercial project. Contact: elmars.builis@gmail.com.

Questions

Is KidMindPath really free?

Yes. All five games are entirely free, with no in-app purchases and no limited free tier. Nothing here is a trial.

Are there ads?

No. None of the five games carries advertising, and none connects to an ad network. The site runs a strict Content-Security-Policy that makes loading anything from a third party technically impossible — including an ad script, even if someone wanted to add one.

Does the site collect data about my child?

No. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel and no third-party script. Every file, fonts included, is served from this same domain. Progress — stars, cards, finished tasks — is written to the browser's local storage on the device and never sent anywhere, because there is no server to receive it.

Does my child need an account?

No. No account, no registration, no email address. The home page lets you enter a child's first name so progress shows up in one place, but that is optional and stays on the device.

Do the games work without an internet connection?

Mostly. Four of the five — Burtu Feja, Mācāmies angliski!, Little Fingers Paint and Ciparu dārzs — cache themselves with a service worker, so they start without a connection after the first visit. Only Prāta piedzīvojumu sala needs a connection each time. Once loaded, none of them sends anything to a server.

Can more than one child use the same device?

Yes. The home page can hold several profiles with different names and ages, and progress is counted separately for each — all of it on that one device and browser.

Is the site available in English?

No. The games and the rest of the site are in Latvian. This page is an English summary of what the site is, not a translation of it.

How do I report a problem?

Email elmars.builis@gmail.com, or use the bug report page (in Latvian).
